Design language

Two layers: a quiet studio, a branded lesson

The authoring chrome is neutral and recedes. All colour and personality belong to the lesson, which is drawn in the workspace theme — so what authors see is what learners get, and the SCORM export uses the same renderer.

Edit the real thingBlocks render as learners see them. No form-per-block; click text to type.
One place for settingsEverything that isn’t content lives in the inspector: layout, style, comments.
On-brand by defaultAuthors pick from theme tokens, never raw colours. Change the theme, every module follows.
Always know who’s hereEach collaborator has a colour; it marks their lesson, their block and their comments.
