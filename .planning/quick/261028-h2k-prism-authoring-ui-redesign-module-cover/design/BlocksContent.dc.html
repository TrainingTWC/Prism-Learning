What counts as personal data?

Any information that identifies a person directly — like a name — or indirectly, when combined with other details we hold.

Direct identifiers name a person on their own: full name, email, phone number, account ID.

Indirect identifiers narrow it down when combined: postcode, job title, purchase history.

If you can tell who it’s about, treat it as personal data.
